WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU SUBMIT
================================================================================

1. Form validates locally (all fields check)
2. Sends POST to http://localhost:5000/api/contact
3. Backend validates again (security)
4. Saves to Supabase database
5. Tries to send notification email (optional, non-blocking)
6. Returns success response
7. Shows "Message sent successfully!" ✅
8. Form resets automatically

The message is saved to database even if email fails!

================================================================================

REQUIRED ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
================================================================================

/backend/.env needs:
  SUPABASE_URL=your_url
  SUPABASE_KEY=your_key
  PORT=5000

/frontend/.env.local needs:
  NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL=http://localhost:5000

If you don't have these, the form won't work!
